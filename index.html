About Abhyas & FAQ

About Abhyas

Abhyas is a Loksewa Civil Engineering exam preparation app for Nepal, designed for Level 7 Engineering candidates. It provides Civil Engineering, General Knowledge, and IQ/reasoning practice questions online and offline.

What is Loksewa?

Lok Sewa Aayog is Nepal's Public Service Commission, which conducts the civil service examinations — including the Level 7 Civil Engineering exam — for government engineering positions.

What does Abhyas cover?

Structural Engineering, Engineering Survey, Construction Materials, Concrete Technology, Geotechnical Engineering, Construction Management, Estimating & Costing, Engineering Drawing, Engineering Economics, and Professional Practices — plus General Knowledge and IQ/reasoning sections that appear alongside the technical paper.

Online and offline practice

Once a chapter or subtopic has been opened while online, its questions are cached on your device for fully offline practice afterward — during load-shedding, on the bus, or anywhere without a connection.

Progress tracking

Accuracy, attempted vs. correct counts, a daily study streak, and weak-topic detection so you always know exactly what to review next — synced across your devices whenever you're online.

Free trial / access

New accounts get a free trial period to explore the app before deciding whether to continue with paid access.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Loksewa (Lok Sewa Aayog)?

Lok Sewa Aayog is Nepal's Public Service Commission, which conducts the civil service examinations — including the Level 7 Civil Engineering exam — for government engineering positions. Abhyas is built specifically to help candidates prepare for this exam.

What subjects does Abhyas cover for Level 7 Civil Engineering?

Structural Engineering, Engineering Survey, Construction Materials, Concrete Technology, Geotechnical Engineering, Construction Management, Estimating & Costing, Engineering Drawing, Engineering Economics, and Professional Practices — plus General Knowledge and IQ/reasoning sections that appear alongside the technical paper.

Can I use Abhyas offline?

Yes. Once you've opened a chapter or subtopic while online, its questions are cached on your device — you can keep practicing the same content with no internet connection, including during load-shedding or while travelling. New or unopened content still needs a connection the first time.

Is Abhyas free?

New accounts get a free trial period to explore the app before deciding whether to continue with paid access.

Does Abhyas track my progress?

Yes — accuracy, attempted vs. correct counts, a daily study streak, and weak-topic detection so you know exactly what to review next, synced across your devices when you're online.
